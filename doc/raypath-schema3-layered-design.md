# The schema3 layered design: geometry objects on u-S² and the demoted MC layer

> Status: **as-built design record** (schema3 report, shipped across the `feat/schema3-report`
> increments — measure layer PR #475, geometry port API v8, report core + serialization, and the
> e2e wiring). This is the durable home of the layering decision that used to live only in the
> redesign discussion notes. The field-by-field contract of the report itself is
> [`raypath-cli-output.md`](./raypath-cli-output.md) §7; the analytic kernel's C surface is
> [`analytic-api.md`](./analytic-api.md). Test scenarios are indexed in
> [`cases/raypath-analysis-cases.md`](./cases/raypath-analysis-cases.md) (C01–C16).

## 1. The decision this document records

The schema-2 report discovered features by Monte-Carlo search over the full pose space and then
tried to *verify* mechanism candidates against those same MC fields. That failed structurally,
not numerically:

- **Semantic mismatch.** MC estimates pushforward observables of the sampling measure; the
  mechanism claim is about an object of the geometry. As N → ∞ the two do not converge to the
  same proposition, so MC can never tell a *wrong attribution* from a right one — every
  at-the-same-δ impostor (gate edge, TIR fold, caustic) produces a similar profile.
- **Discriminating power collapses exactly at the features.** Core-width shifts do not shrink
  with N; smooth-density peaks, zero-finite-support points and sampling-peak drift are
  counterexamples with measured instances (the research history in
  [`feature-diagnostic-discovery-research.md`](./feature-diagnostic-discovery-research.md)
  §9–§19 records the graveyard).
- **Shared frontend.** MC and any geometric layer share config parsing, axis distribution
  semantics, crystal construction, face numbering, Snell and input assembly. A frontend bug
  makes both wrong *and mutually consistent*. The independent oracle must not share that
  frontend (the `fn_period_` hardcode bug was caught precisely because the LI implementation
  forked it).

schema3's answer, owner-ratified: **the geometry layer becomes the primary evidence and MC is
demoted to corroboration.** MC budget starvation drops from a product failure to a declared
verification gap; the default-tier report should be directly usable on the most common paths.

## 2. Two orthogonal layers (and the two corrections that made the cut clean)

The layering is **geometry × measure projection**:

- **Geometry layer** — the constrained critical sets of the chain's deviation map `D_P` and its
  gate/weight structure, *as objects on the orientation-reduced sphere*. Pure geometry: no
  density, no sampling, exact by construction (Newton walks with certificates, closed-form
  values where they exist).
- **Measure projection layer** — everything that turns an object into "what a viewer sees":
  the declared orientation distribution ρ (closed-form for the declared families), visibility
  certificates (measure · area · transmission with non-degenerate jets), weight profiles on
  objects, fiber quadrature for lit ratios.

Two corrections were needed for this split to be honest (they are part of the decision, not
commentary):

- **Correction A — the supporting manifold belongs to the geometry layer; density on it belongs
  to the measure layer.** "Geometry is measure-independent" is imprecise: the core objects are
  support-conditioned (a parhelion is a *restricted* stationary point on the plate support, not
  the intersection of an unrestricted one with a support). Morphology — arc vs spot vs parhelic
  circle — is generated by "the same `D_P` restricted to different supports".
- **Correction B — the Haar-only correctness obligation is gone.** The old obligation came from
  the Haar quotient chart, not from physics. Under the support/u-image charting, a visibility
  certificate is a local exact check under *any declared* measure (μ·A·T > 0 with non-degenerate
  jets, Whitney-type normal form with checkable jet conditions). Haar survives only as a
  computational dividend (quotient structure, I₀ spin averaging, full-circle continuation).

## 3. The u-S² reduction

Fix a chain P (an ordered face sequence). Then the entry-conditions `A_P`, the transmission
product `T_P`, the gate `V_P` and the deviation `D_P` are all functions of the single spherical
variable `u = R⁻¹·ŝ` — the sun direction in the crystal's body frame, where `R` is the crystal
pose. Only the sampling density ρ looks at the full pose; evaluated along a fiber, ρ closes to a
one-dimensional marginal in `u` to ~1.5e-14 (the LI phase-2 measurement). Schema3's scope —
single crystal, single chain — is exactly the validity domain of this reduction.

The geometry layer's objects are therefore three classes of critical lines on u-S², plus the
completion certificate:

- **kind 1** — critical points/values of `D_P` (caustics, halo edges; the per-wavelength
  critical-value table is the n-continuation dispersion). Restricted variant: `D_P` restricted
  to a family's u-image (plate/column/orbit families; the family-support machinery).
- **kind 2** — boundaries of the gate `∂U_P` (where the path stops existing geometrically;
  boundary walks with corner Newton).
- **kind 3** — transmission-weight fold lines (TIR onsets of internal reflections; the
  chromatic typing rides the same walk).
- **Completion certificate** — the critical values cut the δ axis into intervals;
  `TopologyEscape` names the regimes where topology cannot prove completeness (the escape
  regimes; the slug is data, and the report side is fail-closed against unknown ones).

The report-side vocabulary S1–S6 is generated from these (S1 critical sets incl. restricted;
S2 singular sets of the weight = kind-2 + kind-3 + the A=0 corridor; S3 support-boundary
images; S4 branch boundaries — **v1 declares, does not solve**; S5 declared-density feature
images; S6 junctions — **v1 opportunistic + declared non-exhaustive**), plus the measure-layer
roles M1 fiber quadrature (visibility, cross-chain colour ratios), M2 critical-set weight
profiles, M3 declared smoothing (the h/day-plane semantics of the old observation), M4 MC
corroboration and its two-way probe. Closure is argued from the pushed-measure singular-support
decomposition; what is outside the domain is declared, not silently swallowed.

The oracle assignment (owner-ratified, three-layer rationale in §1): **closed-form anchors +
LI cross-validation are the oracle; MC is not.** Concretely: the e2e wiring pins corpus anchors
(C01's closed-form `2·asin(n/2) − 60°`, C05/C06's 120° complement and 149.247° basal-TIR circle,
C11's 108.937° spot) as *exact* numerical facts, and the LI fixtures remain the independent
implementation for anything the two repos both compute.

## 5. S4/S6 v1 scope and the registered gaps

- **S4 (branch boundaries): v1 declares, does not solve.** An `s4_branch_boundary` object or the
  `s4_declared` existence state records that the boundary exists and is out of scope; form-B
  absence certificates declare the S4 scope rather than silently including it.
- **S6 (junctions): opportunistic in v1**, declared non-exhaustive (`s6_junction` objects appear
  where the walks already produce them).
- **G-family ledger (report-core's Step-5 closeout):** G1 (az-Dirac pre-image — registered gap,
  v2 measure layer), G2 (s4_declared cannot ground `unlit` — pinned by test), G3 (escape-regime
  default — owner-raised, slug flows as data meanwhile), G4 (μ-scan cost — measured sub-ms,
  re-evaluate at ≥10⁴-point curves), G5 (producer u-fidelity true; the acos resolution floor
  sets a ≥1e-6 membership tolerance per
  [`numerical-robustness.md`](./numerical-robustness.md)), G6 (non-kArea measures read zero along
  curves — registered gap), G7 (existence routing fail-closed — fixed), G8 (multi-component
  kind-1: per-component certificates aggregated unlit > unproven > partial > certified).

## 6. Two-repo discipline

Lumice = the product (every computation a user can reach, C++, one product implementation per
semantic). Lumice Integral (LI) = research and reference (where new methods are born, and the
independent validation object). The sharing criterion is not "stable ⇒ share" but "stable, **and
not each other's validation object**": primitives and conventions (crystal geometry, face
numbering, symmetry reduction, Snell/Fresnel) are deliberately kept **once per repo** as
independent implementations; algorithm-layer results (single-path inversion, fiber walking)
converge — when mature — into one Lumice C++ implementation shared to LI through the shared
library.

Discipline for every schema3-relevant change: **JAX (LI) is the authority; C++ is the derived
implementation; parity fixtures pin the agreement; a JAX change lands first.** The analytic
kernel's surface (`doc/analytic-api.md`, API v8) is the seam where LI's module-C results enter;
its symmetry semantics are label-vs-physical explicit (`raypath-symmetry.md` §1.1), and parity
fixtures must name which semantics they use.
